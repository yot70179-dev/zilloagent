## When to send

- **Days:** Tuesday–Thursday.
- **Times (agent's US timezone):** 8–10am or 1–3pm.
- **Avoid:** Monday morning, after 5pm, weekends.

---

## 6 rules to stay out of spam

1. Use only **verified** emails (Hunter.io / Apollo.io) — one bounce hurts more than 10 ignores.
2. Send **5–8 per day, manually** — not 20 at once.
3. Personalize every message (name + brokerage in the body).
4. No link in the first line — one clearly-labeled sample link only.
5. Include an opt-out: "Reply STOP and I won't email again." (CAN-SPAM).
6. Warm up a new inbox: 3–5/day the first week, then scale.

---

## How to get verified emails

- **Hunter.io** (free ~25/mo): enter name + company domain → verified email.
- **Apollo.io** (free): similar, with deliverability check.
- **Company contact form / Instagram DM**: safest, zero spam risk.
- Email patterns (verify before sending): KW `first.last@kw.com` · Compass `first.last@compass.com`.
